Where the points come from

Average of each part, 0–100. Overall weights: content 25%, legibility 25%, faithfulness 20%, composition 20%, photo subject 10%.

How each part is measured
Content
How much of the message is still there, not cut off, readable and uncovered. Headline, button, logo, offer and photos count most; legal and decoration least. Losing the headline or the button also cuts the overall score.
Legibility
Share of the original text that draws at 12 px or more, undistorted, uncovered, on a background it can be read on. Text that was cut away counts as unreadable.
Faithfulness
The original's reading order and type hierarchy are kept, and nothing is stretched out of shape.
Composition
No overlaps (each costs a quarter), nothing off the canvas, room at the edges, the canvas used about as fully as the original, and copy kept off the photo subject. Empty letterbox bars scale the whole part down.
Photo subject
How much of each photo's subject (face, person, product) is still visible. A dropped photo counts as 0.
