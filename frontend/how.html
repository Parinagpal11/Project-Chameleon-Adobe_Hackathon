How it works

From one design to every size, in four steps.

This page runs the engine on a real design and shows what it does at each step. Nothing here is a mock-up.

  1. 1

    It reads your design

    Chameleon works out what each element is (headline, button, logo, photo) and finds what matters in the photo: faces, people or the main product.

    • 2

      It decides how much each size has to change

      A size close to your original keeps your layout. A new orientation re-flows it. An extreme shape, like a banner, gets rebuilt.

    • 3

      It tries several layouts and keeps the best one

      For each size it builds alternatives (photo beside or above the copy, bigger type, a framed photo…), scores every one and explains its pick.

      Why this one

      • 4

        It checks every size and proves it beats a plain resize

        Each result is checked for overlaps, edges, text size and contrast, then scored against what stretching or centre-cropping would give.
